Manual Subtitle Extract

Extract a selected embedded text subtitle from a movie or episode as an external SRT sidecar. The item action is added to Jellyfin Web's three-dot menu.

Leave blank to auto-detect Jellyfin FFmpeg or ffmpeg from PATH.
Leave blank to auto-detect Jellyfin FFprobe or ffprobe from PATH.
Disabled by default. Even when enabled, the extraction dialog asks for confirmation before overwriting.
